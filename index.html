Biography

I am an environmental science researcher with a BSc and an MS in Environmental Science from Jashore University of Science and Technology (JUST), Bangladesh. My work centres on microplastics pollution: its fate, transformation and transport in coastal environments, agricultural soils, water, sediments, aquaculture systems and other environmental matrices.

Since February 2022 I have worked part-time as a Research Assistant in the Environmental Laboratory of the Asia Arsenic Network (AAN), Bangladesh. I also study how microplastics interact with emerging contaminants such as per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS), and I am interested in adsorption-based wastewater treatment and biochar-based environmental remediation.

Tracing the Journey of Microplastics in the Northern Bay of Bengal Marine Ecosystems, Bangladesh: A Comprehensive Study on Source Appointments, Distribution, and Potential Risks.

Islam, K. R., Debnath, P. C., Rahman, M. S., et al...

Journal Placeholder Journal of Hazardous Materials Advances 2025 Microplastics
  • Microplastic
  • Ecological risks

This study investigates the presence, characteristics, and ecological risks of microplastics (MPs) in the water, sediment, and biota of Kuakata Sea Beach, Bangladesh. Samples from 12 locations were analysed using sieving, wet peroxide digestion, density separation, optical microscopy, and FTIR spectroscopy. The results revealed significant MP contamination, with higher concentrations in sediment (362.1±50.1 MPs/kg) than in water (145.5±40.7 MPs/L) and biota. Fibers and films were the dominant shapes, with polyethylene (PE) being the most common polymer type. Pollution indices indicated moderate to severe contamination, emphasizing the need for continued monitoring and mitigation efforts. This study provides a crucial baseline for understanding MP pollution in Kuakata and highlights potential ecological risks.
